Snooze Control

Write a roast. Hear it in the app.

Think you can insult a snoozer better than Mom? Pick a character and a snooze, write the line, and send it. The best lines get recorded into the app in that character's voice, credited to you.

This opens your email app with the roast filled in, addressed to support@hearthlyapps.com. Nothing is sent until you press send.

House rules

Roasts are about snoozing, ambition and follow-through. No swearing, and nothing about looks, weight, health, race, religion, gender, sexuality or disability. No real people's names. By sending a line you let Hearthly Apps record it into Snooze Control and use it in the app's videos; we credit you if you ask us to.
