Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my Sustain+ subscription?
Subscriptions are billed and managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap "Manage subscription" inside Sustain's Settings tab.

Is my health data private?
Yes. Your logs are stored on your device, and we don't sell or share your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Does Sustain replace medical advice?
No. Sustain is a personal tracking tool, not a diagnostic or treatment device. Always talk to your prescriber about your treatment plan, dose changes, or any side effects.

How do I restore a purchase after reinstalling?
Open the paywall in Sustain and tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom.
