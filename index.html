How we build

Three rules, and what each one costs us.

Principles are cheap when they're free. These are the ones we've actually paid for.

Rule 01

The free tier is free forever.

Not a trial with a countdown. In Sustain, logging your dose, weight, protein and side effects costs nothing and always will. Only the AI layer is paid.

The cost: most of our users will never pay us anything, and we built the pricing knowing that.

Rule 02

Your health data is not the product.

It is never sold, never handed to advertisers, and never used to train an AI model. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, permanently, without emailing anyone.

The cost: the entire revenue model that most free health apps run on is closed to us.

Rule 03

One magic moment, not AI everywhere.

We'd rather ship a single AI feature that genuinely changes the workflow than sprinkle a chatbot over every screen. In Sustain that moment is photographing a plate and getting the protein back.

The cost: a shorter feature list than every competitor, on purpose.
