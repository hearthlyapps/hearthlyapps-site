What actually happens

Nobody decides to lose muscle. It just isn't the thing being measured.

Rapid weight loss without enough protein costs you lean mass as well as fat. Most of the time the sequence looks like this, and every step of it feels like progress.

The dose goes up

How it works

Six screens you'd actually open on a Tuesday.

01 — Today

Dose, protein and weight on one screen.

Not three apps and a notes file. Your next dose, today's protein against a target set for your body, and where your weight is actually heading, in a single glance before you put the phone down.

02 — AI photo estimate

Point the camera at dinner. Get the protein.

No database searching, no weighing, no guessing at portions. Snap the plate and Sustain estimates the protein in seconds. It shows its work and you can edit the number, because an estimate you can't correct is just a guess with better manners.

03 — Ask Sustain

Ask about your data, not the internet's.

"How's my protein trending this week?" gets an answer built from what you actually logged. It never diagnoses, never reads a lab value and never suggests a dose. Those conversations belong to your prescriber, and Sustain is built to send you back to them better prepared.

04 — Trends

See where your dose is, not just where your weight is.

Sustain estimates your medication level from the doses you've logged and your medication's published half-life, and plots it against your weight trend, so a rough week has context instead of being a mystery.

05 — Body composition

The chart that answers the actual question.

Connect a compatible smart scale through Apple Health and watch body fat and lean mass move separately. This is the whole point: two lines where the scale only ever gave you one.

06 — Maintenance

And a plan for the day the number stops falling.

Hitting your goal isn't the end of the story, it's the part nobody prepared you for. Sustain's maintenance program gives you a range to hold instead of a number to chase, and the questions worth bringing to your next appointment.

Before you download

The parts people ask about first.

"Free" usually means free for a week.

Not here. Logging your dose, weight, protein and side effects is free forever, with no card and no countdown. Sustain+ unlocks the AI features and your full history. That's the whole split.

Where does my health data go?

To your own account, protected so only you can read it. It is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used to train an AI model. You can permanently delete the account and its data from inside the app.

Is this giving me medical advice?

No, deliberately. Sustain never diagnoses, never interprets a lab value and never suggests a dose change. It transcribes, tracks and shows you patterns. Every clinical decision stays with your prescriber.

Does it work with what I already use?

Weight and protein sync both ways with Apple Health, so you're not logging the same thing twice. There's a Lock Screen widget, a Live Activity and an Apple Watch complication for logging a dose in one tap.
